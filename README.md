# Poker VPN

Poker VPN is built for developers in Iran and other countries where sanctions restrict access to the open internet. It is also designed for the general public and is free to use.

> Here’s to a day when no developer has to bypass sanctions to exercise their right to a free and open internet.

## Intended Use

Poker VPN is designed to help developers and the general public access the open internet. It is not intended to circumvent or violate the laws of any country. Please follow the laws that apply where you use it.

## VPN Core

Poker VPN uses [Xray-core](https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core) as its VPN engine.
